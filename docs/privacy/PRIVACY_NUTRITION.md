## Data Usage Purposes

### Essential Services
- **Account Management**: Creating and managing user accounts
- **Service Delivery**: Providing security services and guard dispatch
- **Emergency Response**: Responding to emergency situations
- **Payment Processing**: Processing payments for services
- **Communication**: Sending service-related notifications

### Service Improvement
- **Analytics**: Understanding how users interact with the app
- **Performance Monitoring**: Identifying and fixing app issues
- **Feature Development**: Developing new features based on usage patterns
- **Quality Assurance**: Ensuring service quality and reliability

### Legal Compliance
- **Record Keeping**: Maintaining records for legal and regulatory purposes
- **Audit Trails**: Creating audit trails for security and compliance
- **Dispute Resolution**: Resolving disputes and complaints
- **Regulatory Reporting**: Meeting regulatory reporting requirements
