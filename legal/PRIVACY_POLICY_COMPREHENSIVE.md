# SwiftGuard Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## 1. Introduction

SwiftGuard ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services (the "Platform").

This policy applies to all users of SwiftGuard, including clients seeking security services and security guards providing services through our platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
We collect information you provide directly, including:

**For All Users:**
- Name, email address, phone number
- Date of birth and government-issued ID
- Profile photos and uploaded documents
- Payment information (processed securely through Stripe)
- Communication preferences

**For Guards:**
- Professional licenses and certifications
- Background check results
- Emergency contact information
- Work experience and qualifications
- Bank account information for payments

**For Clients:**
- Business information and registration details
- Billing address and payment methods
- Emergency contact information

### 2.2 Location Information
**Critical for Safety and Service Delivery:**
- **Real-time GPS location** during active security assignments
- **Check-in/check-out locations** for job verification
- **Emergency location data** for rapid response
- **Geofencing data** for checkpoint verification

**Location data collection stops when guards are off-duty**

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information
- Device information (model, operating system, unique identifiers)
- App usage patterns and performance metrics
- IP address and network information
- Time stamps and interaction logs

### 2.4 Communication Data
- In-app messages between clients and guards
- Incident reports and emergency communications
- Customer support interactions
- Photo and video evidence from security incidents
